How to play

The rules

  • Fill every cell with a number from 1 to 9.
  • Each number appears once in every row, every column and every 3×3 box.
  • Printed numbers belong to the puzzle and can’t be changed. Your numbers appear in handwriting.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Playing

  1. Tap a cell to select it. Its row, column and box light up, along with every matching number.
  2. Tap a number to place it. Tap the same number again to take it back.
  3. Turn on Notes to pencil in candidates. Placing a number clears it from the notes around it.
  4. Undo steps back one move. Erase clears the selected cell.
  5. A number is crossed out once all 9 of it are on the board.

Hints

A hint fills the selected cell, or a random empty cell if none is selected. Each puzzle gets a fixed number:

Easy: 5
Medium: 3
Hard: 2
Expert: 1

Levels and streaks

  • Every solved puzzle raises your level by one.
  • Solve puzzles back to back to build a streak. Abandoning a puzzle you’ve started resets it.
  • Streak badges: ✨ 1+, 🌟 3+, ⚡ 5+, 🔥 10+.
  • Your best time is kept for each size and difficulty.

Tips

  • Start with the numbers that appear most often.
  • Look for rows, columns or boxes that are nearly full.
  • If a cell has only one possible number left, that’s your move.
  • The game pauses on its own when you switch away.
